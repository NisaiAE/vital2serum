vital2serum is a modified work. It was written in 2026 as a converter from
Vital to Serum 2, and contains material ported from and derived from the two
works below. Both are licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later.


1. Vital

   Copyright 2013-2019 Matt Tytel
   https://github.com/mtytel/vital
   Commit: 636ca0ef517a4db087a6a08a6a8a5e704e21f836

   vital2serum/wavetable.py is a Python port, with changes, of the wavetable
   rendering in
     src/common/wavetable/wavetable_creator.cpp
     src/common/wavetable/wavetable_group.cpp
     src/common/wavetable/wavetable_component.cpp
     src/common/wavetable/wavetable_keyframe.cpp
     src/common/wavetable/wave_source.cpp
     src/common/wavetable/wave_line_source.cpp
     src/common/wavetable/file_source.cpp
     src/common/wavetable/wave_warp_modifier.cpp
     src/common/wavetable/wave_fold_modifier.cpp
     src/common/wavetable/frequency_filter_modifier.cpp
     src/common/wavetable/phase_modifier.cpp
     src/common/wavetable/wave_window_modifier.cpp
     src/common/line_generator.cpp
   and its oscillator warps and spectral morphs follow
     src/synthesis/producers/synth_oscillator.cpp

   vital2serum/spectral.py uses the full-table spectral morph, phase and
   amplitude formulas from
     src/synthesis/producers/spectral_morph.h
     src/synthesis/lookups/wavetable.cpp
     src/synthesis/framework/futils.h

   vital2serum/ranges.py and vital2serum/defaults.py hold parameter ranges and
   defaults from
     src/common/synth_parameters.cpp

   vital2serum/curves.py and vital2serum/modulation.py use formulas from
     src/synthesis/framework/futils.h
     src/synthesis/modules/modulation_connection_processor.cpp


2. serum2vital

   Copyright (C) the serum2vital authors
   https://github.com/btesser/serum2vital
   Commit: b67d9d3d0b8e03c9930d6b8f81eca0e4a9560e35

   The description of the Serum 2 preset container, the Vital parameter
   table, mapping tables and several conversion laws are derived from it, in
   the opposite direction:
     vital2serum/serum.py, vital2serum/mapping.py, vital2serum/modulation.py,
     vital2serum/effects.py, vital2serum/defaults.py

   The bundled file
     vital2serum/data/Init-2.0.24.SerumPreset
   is an unmodified copy of that repository's
     DebugPresets/1.SerumPreset
     Git blob SHA-1: e68328daa7fb52892ebff9cad28cc512f31e5913
   It is a preset saved from the initial state of Serum 2.0.24. It holds
   default parameter state and the names of stock files, and no wavetable or
   sample audio. A preset saved by another copy of Serum can be used in its
   place with --template.

   serum2vital is not the author or endorser of this converter.
